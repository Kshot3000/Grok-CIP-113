## Community introduction

Hi Cardano builders — I’m KShot (@kshot9000). I’ve released PRISM, an open-source design and research workspace around CIP-113 programmable tokens.

You can configure a token design, exercise transfer denial paths, export a portable manifest, model a private-credit scenario, connect a Cardano or Midnight wallet, look up Cardano assets, and derive a smart-wallet address from your owner credential.

The release clearly distinguishes actual wallet/data utilities from local models. It does not mint tokens, execute a lending agreement, or deploy a Midnight proof bridge. I’d welcome feedback and contributors interested in reviewed testnet transaction flows, verified registry discovery, and privacy-preserving eligibility.
